Read WattTime’s formal complaint here : Scientific Accuracy Complaint Against the Greenhouse Gas Protocol’s Proposed Scope 2 Update: The Need for Additionality In collaboration with leading researchers on carbon accounting, WattTime submitted a formal complaint to the Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHGP) on January 7, 2026, challenging the scientific merit of the emissions reductions claims underlying its Scope 2 proposal for deliverable, hourly matching.
